AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF AMERICAN CANYON ADDING CHAPTER 13.08 TO TITLE 13 OF THE AMERICAN CANYON MUNICIPAL CODE RELATING TO BACKFLOW PREVENTION AND CROSS-CONNECTION CONTROL; AMENDING CHAPTER 13.04 RELATING TO WATER SERVICE SYSTEMS; AMENDING CHAPTER 13.06 RELATING TO WATER RATES AND CONNECTION FEES; AMENDING SECTION 16.14.080 RELATING TO WATER EFFICIENT LANDSCAPING; AMENDING SECTION 19.51.060 RELATING TO STANDARD CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL; AMENDING TITLE 14 RELATING TO SEWER AND SANITATION SERVICES; AND AMENDING TITLE 1 RELATING TO GENERAL PROVISIONS

Ord. No. 2026-01. AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF AMERICAN CANYON ADDING CHAPTER 13.08 TO TITLE 13 OF THE AMERICAN CANYON MUNICIPAL CODE RELATING TO BACKFLOW PREVENTION AND CROSS-CONNECTION CONTROL; AMENDING CHAPTER 13.04 RELATING TO WATER SERVICE SYSTEMS; AMENDING CHAPTER 13.06 RELATING TO WATER RATES AND CONNECTION FEES; AMENDING SECTION 16.14.080 RELATING TO WATER EFFICIENT LANDSCAPING; AMENDING SECTION 19.51.060 RELATING TO STANDARD CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL; AMENDING TITLE 14 RELATING TO SEWER AND SANITATION SERVICES; AND AMENDING TITLE 1 RELATING TO GENERAL PROVISIONS

WHEREAS, the City of American Canyon owns and operates a public water system that provides potable water service to residents, businesses, institutions, and other customers within the City; and

WHEREAS, the State Water Resources Control Board has adopted the Cross-Connection Control Policy Handbook ("CCCPH") pursuant to California Health and Safety Code section 116407 to establish standards and requirements for backflow protection and cross-connection control applicable to California public water systems; and

WHEREAS, the CCCPH establishes requirements for public water systems to implement and maintain effective cross-connection control programs to protect public water systems from contamination or pollution resulting from backflow and cross-connections; and

WHEREAS, cross-connections and backflow conditions present a risk to potable water systems by allowing contaminants from private plumbing systems, irrigation systems, fire suppression systems, auxiliary water supplies, and industrial or commercial processes to enter the public water supply; and

WHEREAS, protection of the City's potable water system is necessary to preserve public health, safety, and welfare and to ensure continued compliance with applicable State and federal drinking water requirements; and

WHEREAS, the City currently administers backflow prevention requirements through conditions of approval, engineering standards, and administrative practices; however, the City Council finds that establishing a comprehensive and codified program will improve consistency, enforceability, regulatory compliance, and long-term program effectiveness; and

WHEREAS, the City Council finds that adoption of uniform standards for the installation, testing, maintenance, inspection, certification, reporting, recordkeeping, and enforcement of backflow prevention assemblies is necessary to ensure a reliable and effective cross-connection control program; and

WHEREAS, the City Council finds that adoption of a comprehensive Backflow Prevention and Cross-Connection Control Program, codified as Chapter 13.08 of the American Canyon Municipal Code, is necessary to comply with applicable State requirements and protect the City's potable water system; and

WHEREAS, the City Council desires to amend related provisions of the American Canyon Municipal Code concerning water service, water rates and connection fees, irrigation, development conditions, sewer and sanitation services, and general enforcement to ensure consistency with Chapter 13.08; and

WHEREAS, the City Council has considered the requirements of the California Environmental Quality Act ("CEQA"), Public Resources Code section 21000 et seq., and finds that adoption of this Ordinance is exempt from CEQA pursuant to CEQA Guidelines section 15061(b)(3), because it can be seen with certainty that there is no possibility that adoption of the Ordinance, which establishes administrative and regulatory requirements for backflow prevention and cross-connection control to protect the City's potable water system, may have a significant effect on the environment; and

WHEREAS, on August 18, 2026, the City Council considered the proposed Ordinance, received the staff presentation and public comment, and introduced the Ordinance for first reading; and

WHEREAS, on September 1, 2026, the City Council considered the Ordinance for second reading and final adoption.

NOW, THEREFORE, THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF AMERICAN CANYON DOES ORDAIN AS FOLLOWS:

Section 1. ADOPTION AND PROGRAM ADMINISTRATION. Chapter 13.08 of Title 13 of the American Canyon Municipal Code, titled "Backflow Prevention and Cross-Connection Control," is hereby added as set forth in Exhibit A, attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference.

The City Council authorizes the Public Works Director, or designee, to administer and enforce the Backflow Prevention and Cross-Connection Control Program in accordance with this Ordinance, applicable City Council resolutions, and applicable State law and regulations.

Section 2. RELATED MUNICIPAL CODE AMENDMENTS. The related amendments to the American Canyon Municipal Code set forth in Exhibits B through G are hereby adopted and incorporated herein by reference.

Section 3. CEQA. The City Council finds that adoption of this Ordinance is exempt from the California Environmental Quality Act ("CEQA") pursuant to CEQA Guidelines section 15061(b)(3), the common-sense exemption, because it can be seen with certainty that there is no possibility that adoption of this Ordinance may have a significant effect on the environment.

The Ordinance establishes administrative and regulatory requirements relating to backflow prevention and cross-connection control, including program administration, installation, testing, maintenance, reporting, recordkeeping, inspection, and enforcement requirements intended to protect the City's potable water system.

Section 4. EFFECTIVE DATE. This Ordinance shall take effect thirty (30) days following its adoption. The City Clerk shall cause this Ordinance, or a summary thereof, to be published in accordance with applicable law.

Section 5. SEVERABILITY. If any section, subsection, sentence, clause, phrase, or portion of this Ordinance is for any reason held to be invalid or unconstitutional by a decision of a court of competent jurisdiction, such decision shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions of this Ordinance.

The City Council hereby declares that it would have adopted this Ordinance and each section, subsection, sentence, clause, phrase, or portion thereof irrespective of the fact that any one or more sections, subsections, sentences, clauses, phrases, or portions may be declared invalid or unconstitutional.

Section 6. CUSTODIAN OF RECORDS. The documents and materials that constitute the record of proceedings upon which this Ordinance is based are located at the City Clerk's Office, 4831 Broadway, Suite 201, American Canyon, California 94503. The custodian of these records is the City Clerk.

EXHIBIT A

CHAPTER 13.08 - BACKFLOW PREVENTION AND CROSS-CONNECTION CONTROL ORDINANCE

Section 13.08.010. PURPOSE AND INTENT

This document establishes the administrative procedures, technical standards, and fee schedule for implementing Chapter 13.08 of the American Canyon Municipal Code, "Backflow Prevention and Cross-Connection Control."

The intent is to ensure that all backflow prevention assemblies connected to the City's potable water system are properly installed, tested, and maintained to protect public health and comply with California Code of Regulations, Title 17, Sections 7583-7605.

Section 13.08.020. APPLICABILITY

This ordinance applies to any property owner that receives potable water service by the City of American Canyon and requires a new backflow prevention and cross-control device ("device") on their property or owns an existing device on their property.

Section 13.08.030. PROGRAM ADMINISTRATION

A. The Water Division within the Public Works Department shall administer the program under the authority of the Public Works Director or designee.

B. The Program Administrator shall:

1. Maintain a database of all registered backflow prevention assemblies.

2. Maintain a current list of certified testers.

3. Track annual test results and non-compliance notices.

4. Coordinate enforcement actions with Utility Billing and Code Enforcement.

Section 13.08.040. APPROVED DEVICES

A. Only assemblies approved by the University of Southern California Foundation for Cross-Connection Control and Hydraulic Research (USC-FCCCHR) and accepted by the City Engineer shall be installed. The City Engineer shall maintain and update the Approved Assembly List consistent with City Standard Details and Code Section 18.42.010 authority.

B. Device types include:

1. Air Gap Separation (AG)

2. Reduced Pressure Principal Assembly (RP)

3. Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA)

4. Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB)

C. The Public Works Director may approve equivalent devices upon proof of listing and performance testing.

D. The Water Division shall publish and maintain an Approved Assembly List, updated annually.

Section 13.08.050. CERTIFIED TESTERS

A. Certification Requirements

Testers must:

1. Hold a valid certification from AWWA or American Backflow Prevention Association (ABPA).

2. Provide proof of calibration of test equipment (within past 12 months).

3. Maintain a City of American Canyon business license.

4. Submit an application to be added to the City's Certified Tester List.

B. Renewal

Tester approval is valid for one calendar year and must be renewed annually with updated certification and equipment calibration documentation.

C. Conduct

Certified testers must perform all testing in accordance with AWWA Manual M14 and report findings honestly and accurately. Any falsified report shall result in immediate removal from the City's approved list and possible referral to regulatory authorities.

Section 13.08.060. TESTING AND REPORTING REQUIREMENTS

A. Initial Testing

1. Every new backflow prevention assembly serving a new building, new water service, or new facility shall be tested and certified by the City prior to final occupancy or water service activation.

B. Annual Testing

1. Annually thereafter, the property owner shall be responsible for ensuring that all required backflow prevention assembly testing is completed by a Certified Backflow Prevention Assembly Tester approved by the City and that all required test reports are submitted to the Water Division in accordance with this Chapter.

2. The Water Division shall send annual notifications to property owners at least thirty (30) days prior to the testing due date. The Public Works Director, or designee, may exercise reasonable administrative discretion in the enforcement of testing deadlines when circumstances warrant, including when additional time is reasonably necessary to complete repairs, retesting, or other corrective actions, provided such discretion is consistent with this Chapter and applicable State regulations.

C. Reporting

1. Test reports must be submitted by the property owner to the City prior to the annual due date and within ten (10) business days after testing using the City-approved Backflow Test Report Form.

2. Reports may be submitted electronically via the City's online portal or by mail to the Water Division.

3. Only test reports completed by a Certified Backflow Prevention Assembly Tester approved by the City shall be accepted. Reports may be submitted in paper or electronic format and shall bear either an original handwritten signature or an authenticated electronic signature of the certified tester. The Public Works Director, or designee, may establish administrative procedures by resolution for the electronic submission, verification, and acceptance of test reports.

D. Failed Tests

1. If an assembly fails testing, it must be repaired or replaced within ten (10) calendar days.

2. A re-test must be performed and reported within five (5) business days after repair.

E. Non-Compliance

Failure to comply with testing or repair deadlines will result in:

1. Issuance of a Notice of Violation (NOV);

2. Application of a Non-Compliance Administrative Fee; and

3. Potential termination of water service until compliance is achieved.

Section 13.08.070. INSTALLATION REQUIREMENTS

1. Installation shall conform to City Standard Details and applicable plumbing codes.

2. Assemblies shall not be buried or installed in pits subject to flooding.

3. Assemblies shall be installed in an approved security cage or enclosure meeting City Standard Details.

4. Vault installations are not permitted unless specifically approved by the City Engineer.

5. The customer is responsible for maintenance of the installation area, including keeping enclosures accessible and free from vegetation or obstructions.

6. Fire protection systems shall install assemblies approved for fire-line use per AWWA C510/C511 and NFPA standards.

Section 13.08.080. RECORDKEEPING

The Water Division shall:

1. Maintain records necessary to administer and enforce the City's Backflow Prevention and Cross-Connection Control Program. The Water Division shall maintain digital records of all backflow prevention assemblies, including their locations, owners, and test results, for a minimum period of five (5) years.

2. Recordkeeping procedures, including requirements for documentation, retention periods, reporting, and maintenance of records related to backflow prevention assemblies, test results, and program activities, shall be established and adopted by City Council Resolution.

Section 13.08.090. FEES

The following fees shall be established and may be updated annually by City Council Resolution. Any annual adjustment to the fees shall be based on the Municipal Cost Index published by American City & County, or a successor agency or publication, as the identified inflation adjustment factor. The City Council may consider additional factors necessary to ensure that fees remain consistent with the reasonable costs associated with administering the Backflow Prevention and Cross-Connection Control Program.

Fee Type Description Amount*
Annual Program Administration Fee Charged per registered backflow assembly to recover administrative costs. Established by City Council Resolution
Late Test Report Fee Charged if annual test report is not received by due date. Established by City Council Resolution
Non-Compliance Reinspection Fee Charged for each follow-up inspection by City staff. Established by City Council Resolution
Certified Tester Application Fee Annual fee to maintain listing on approved tester list. Established by City Council Resolution
Backflow Prevention Assembly Installation Permit Fee Fee for review, approval, inspection, documentation, and administration associated with the initial installation, relocation, replacement, or modification of a backflow prevention assembly. Fees may vary based on the number of assemblies, assembly type, size, complexity, and level of review required. Established by City Council Resolution
Backflow Prevention Assembly Annual Testing Fee (if applicable) Fee associated with administration of the annual backflow prevention assembly testing program, including record management, compliance tracking, and reporting requirements. Established by City Council Resolution
Backflow Prevention Assembly Reinspection Fee (if applicable) Fee for additional inspections, reviews, or administrative efforts required due to failed tests, incomplete documentation, non-compliance, or corrective actions. Established by City Council Resolution

Section 13.08.100. ENFORCEMENT PROCEDURE

1. Notice of Violation (NOV): will be issued to the property owner responsible for the backflow prevention assembly. The NOV shall identify the violation, required corrective action, and compliance timeframe. The NOV may be served by mail, electronic delivery, personal delivery, or other method approved by the Public Works Director or designee. The compliance timeframe shall account for delivery of the notice and the time reasonably necessary to complete corrective actions, including repairs and retesting. The Public Works Director, or designee, may extend compliance deadlines when warranted.

2. Second Notice: The Public Works Director, or designee, may issue a Notice of Violation (NOV) requiring corrective action. If compliance is not achieved within fifteen (15) days of the date of the NOV for electronic delivery, or the postmarked date of the NOV for mailed delivery, or within another timeframe specified in the NOV, the City may pursue additional enforcement actions as authorized by this Chapter. If a violation remains unresolved and poses a threat to public health or the quality of the City's water supply, the City may perform or arrange for the repair or replacement of the backflow prevention assembly as necessary to protect the public water system. The property owner shall be responsible for reimbursement of all reasonable costs incurred by the City associated with such corrective actions. Fees established pursuant to this Chapter shall be based on the reasonable costs incurred by the City for administering, implementing, and enforcing the Backflow Prevention and Cross-Connection Control Program. Fee amounts may be based on factors including, but not limited to, staff time, inspection requirements, recordkeeping, reporting, testing administration, program management, and other costs necessary to provide the applicable service. Fees shall be established and updated by City Council Resolution and shall not exceed the reasonable cost of providing the associated service or administering the program

3. Final Notice / Shutoff Warning: If still noncompliant after 30 days from the initial NOV issuance date, water service may be terminated per AMC §13.08.090.

4. Reinstatement: Service restored upon receipt of passing test report and payment of applicable fees.

Section 13.08.110. ADMINISTRATIVE APPEALS

A. Customers may appeal any enforcement action when supported by substantial information under AMC §13.08.100 to the City Manager within ten (10) days of a NOV issuance. The decision of the City Manager is final.

Section 13.08.120. FORMS AND REFERENCES

A. The following forms and references are adopted by reference and maintained by the Water Division:

1. City of American Canyon Backflow Test Report Form.

2. Certified Tester Application Form.

3. USC-FCCCHR Approved Assemblies List.

4. City of American Canyon Standard Detail Drawings for Backflow Assemblies.

5. AWWA Manual M14 – Recommended Practice for Backflow Prevention and Cross-Connection Control.

B. Forms, standard details, and administrative documents, provided such updates do not amend the substantive requirements of Chapter 13.08, may be updated by the Public Works Director or designee without further City Council action.

EXHIBIT B AMENDMENTS TO AMERICAN CANYON MUNICIPAL CODE SECTION 13.04 – WATER SERVICE REGULATIONS

Section 13.04 of the American Canyon Municipal Code is hereby amended to add the following:

All new water service connections, and any modifications to existing water service connections that require a building permit, encroachment permit, or discretionary or ministerial development review approval, shall comply with Chapter 13.08, Backflow Prevention and Cross-Connection Control.

Existing water service connections that are not subject to modification, redevelopment, or permit review may remain in their current condition. If future construction, redevelopment, change in use, or permit activity triggers City review, the water service shall be brought into compliance with Chapter 13.08 as a condition of approval.

All applicable program fees, including inspection fees, testing fees, and administrative fees associated with Chapter 13.08, shall be assessed at the time of permit issuance or development review.

EXHIBIT C AMENDMENTS TO AMERICAN CANYON MUNICIPAL CODE SECTION 13.06 – WATER RATES AND CHARGES

Section 13.06 is hereby amended to add the following provision:

The City Council may establish by resolution an annual backflow assembly testing administration fee for each backflow prevention assembly subject to inspection, testing, or compliance under Chapter 13.08. The fee shall be established to recover the reasonable costs of program administration, inspection coordination, and compliance oversight.

EXHIBIT D AMENDMENTS TO AMERICAN CANYON MUNICIPAL CODE SECTION 16.14.080 – WATER EFFICIENT LANDSCAPING

Section 16.14.080 is hereby amended to read as follows:

All irrigation systems shall include an approved backflow prevention assembly. The assembly shall be installed and maintained in accordance with Chapter 13.08,Backflow Prevention and Cross-Connection Control.

EXHIBIT E AMENDMENTS TO AMERICAN CANYON MUNICIPAL CODE SECTION 19.54.060 – STANDARD CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL

Section 19.54.060, Condition of Approval No. (12), is hereby amended to read as follows:

All water services, including domestic, irrigation, and fire services, shall be equipped with approved backflow prevention assemblies. The assemblies shall be installed, tested, and maintained in accordance with Chapter 13.08 prior to final occupancy approval.

EXHIBIT F AMENDMENTS TO TITLE 14 – UNCODIFIED PUBLIC WORKS STANDARDS

Title 14 of the American Canyon Municipal Code is hereby amended to add a new uncodified policy to require that all standard plans, specifications, and construction details for water system improvements be updated to reflect the requirements of Chapter 13.08.

These updates shall include, but are not limited to, standards for backflow prevention assembly installation, vault sizing and placement, clearance requirements, access requirements, and approved device types consistent with applicable state and industry standards.

All water system improvements shall be constructed in accordance with these updated standards.

EXHIBIT G AMENDMENTS TO TITLE 1 – GENERAL PENALTY AND ENFORCEMENT

Title 1 of the American Canyon Municipal Code is hereby amended to add the following as a new section::

Section 1.24.060:

Any violation of Chapter 13.08 is unlawful and may be enforced as an infraction, misdemeanor, or by administrative citation in accordance with City enforcement procedures. Each day a violation continues shall constitute a separate and distinct offense.

The remedies provided herein are cumulative and not exclusive.
