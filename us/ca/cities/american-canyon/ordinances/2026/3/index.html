Ord. No. 2026-03. AN ORDINANCE AMENDING AMERICAN CANYON MUNICIPAL CODE SECTIONS 19.05.090, 19.05.100, 19.10.040, AND 19.31.020 TO REMOVE THE PROHIBITION ON SWINE, LIMIT LIVESTOCK KEEPING TO THE RESIDENTIAL ESTATE (RE) ZONING DISTRICT, AND REMOVE OBSOLETE GENERAL PLAN POLICY REFERENCES

WHEREAS, the American Canyon Municipal Code (ACMC) regulates livestock keeping and animal husbandry through Chapter 19.05 (Use Classifications), Chapter 19.10 (Residential Zoning Districts), and Chapter 19.31 (Animal Keeping Standards); and

WHEREAS, ACMC Sections 19.05.090 and 19.05.100 currently exclude swine from the definitions of animal husbandry and livestock keeping; and

WHEREAS, the proposed amendments would remove those exclusions and allow swine where animal husbandry or livestock keeping are otherwise permitted by the ACMC; and

WHEREAS, The Residential Estate (RE) zoning district is intended for low-density residential development on large lots where livestock keeping is compatible with the area's rural character. In contrast, the Rural Residential Hillside (RRH), Rural Residential (RR), and Residential Single-Family (RS) zoning districts are intended primarily for residential use and generally have development patterns or lot sizes that make livestock keeping less compatible with surrounding neighborhoods.; and

WHEREAS, the proposed amendments would clarify that livestock keeping is allowed as an accessory use only in the Residential Estate (RE) zoning district. Livestock-keeping uses that were legally established in other residential zoning districts may continue in accordance with the Municipal Code as legal nonconforming uses; and

WHEREAS, the proposed amendments would clarify that animal husbandry is permitted only in the Residential Estate (RE) zoning district, as animal husbandry is generally a rural agricultural use that is most appropriate on larger lots with greater separation from nearby homes. Any animal husbandry uses that were legally established in other residential zoning districts may continue as legal nonconforming uses; and

WHEREAS, ACMC Chapter 19.31 establishes standards for animal keeping, including minimum lot sizes, setbacks, maintenance requirements, sanitation standards, nuisance prevention measures, and enforcement provisions, all of which would remain in effect; and

WHEREAS, the proposed amendments primarily remove the prohibition on swine and update zoning regulations while retaining all existing animal-keeping standards and operational requirements; and

WHEREAS, the proposed amendments would remove obsolete General Plan policy references currently contained in ACMC Section 19.10.040; and

WHEREAS, the proposed amendments are consistent with the General Plan because they support animal keeping and agricultural uses in appropriate locations while maintaining compatibility with surrounding properties through existing Municipal Code standards; and

WHEREAS, on July 30, 2026, the Planning Commission of the City of American Canyon conducted a public hearing and unanimously recommended City Council approval of the ordinance; and

WHEREAS, a duly noticed public hearing was held by the City Council of the City of American Canyon on September 1, 2026, at which time all interested persons were given an opportunity to be heard regarding the proposed Municipal Code amendments.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED that the City Council of the City of American Canyon hereby adopts amendments to the American Canyon Municipal Code as follows:

Section 1. Amendment to ACMC Section 19.05.090 – Animal Husbandry

Amend the definition of Animal Husbandry to remove the prohibition on swine as follows:

Animal Husbandry: Raising of animals or production of animal products, such as eggs or dairy products, on an agricultural or commercial basis. This classification includes grazing, ranching, dairy farming, poultry raising, the raising of fur-bearing animals, the raising of swine, and the sale and storage of materials produced on-site, but excludes feed lots, stockyards, slaughterhouses, dead animal rendering, and wild animal keeping.

Section 2. Amendment to ACMC Section 19.05.100 – Livestock Keeping

Amend the definition of Livestock Keeping to allow swine as follows:

Livestock Keeping: The keeping of large animals for personal purposes, including equines, bovines, sheep, goats, and swine in accordance with the provisions of Chapter 19.31, Animal Keeping Regulations.

Section 3. Amendment to ACMC Section 19.31.020(B)(2) – Animal Keeping Standards

Amend the livestock provisions to allow swine as follows:

(B) Permitted Animals

(2) Livestock. Large animals, including equines, bovines, sheep, goats, and swine, may be kept for personal purposes where livestock keeping is permitted as an accessory use in the underlying zoning district.

Section 4. Amendment to ACMC Section 19.10.040 – Allowable Uses Table

Amend ACMC Section 19.10.040 to:

1. Permit livestock keeping as an accessory use only in the Residential Estate (RE) zoning district;

2. Clarify where animal husbandry is permitted;

3. Continue recognition of legally established nonconforming uses as provided by the Municipal Code; and

4. Remove obsolete General Plan policy references as shown on the table below:

Section 5. CEQA Findings The City Council finds that the proposed Municipal Code amendments are exempt from environmental review under the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA) Guidelines Section 15061(b)(3) (General Rule) because it is clear that the amendments will not have a significant effect on the environment.

The amendments do not allow new development, increase development density, expand the areas where livestock keeping or animal husbandry are permitted, or remove existing rules for animal keeping. Instead, the amendments remove the prohibition on swine in certain existing use classifications, clarify and in some cases limit where livestock keeping and animal husbandry are allowed, and continue to apply all existing standards related to lot size, setbacks, maintenance, sanitation, nuisance prevention, and code enforcement. Because the amendments will not cause a direct or reasonably foreseeable indirect physical change to the environment, they are exempt under CEQA Guidelines Section 15061(b)(3).

The City Council also finds that the amendments are exempt under CEQA Guidelines Section 15305 (Minor Alterations in Land Use Limitations) because they make only minor changes to land use regulations and do not change land use designations, allowed density, or development intensity.

Section 6. Effective Date. This ordinance shall become effective 30 days after its final passage pursuant to Government Code section 36937.

Section 7. Severability. If any section, sentence, clause or phrase of this Ordinance is for any reason held to be invalid or unconstitutional by a decision of any court of competent jurisdiction, such decision shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions of this Ordinance. The City Council hereby declares that it would have passed this Ordinance and adopted this Ordinance and each section, sentence, clause or phrase thereof, irrespective of the fact that one or more sections, subsections, sentences, clauses or phrases be declared invalid or unconstitutional.
